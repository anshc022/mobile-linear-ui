# Fasal Seva — NIC Codes & Business Registration Guide

## Quick Summary for Team

Fasal Seva needs to be registered as a startup with the right NIC (National Industrial Classification) codes. These codes tell the government what our business does. Since we're building an **AI-powered farming app + IoT sensor device**, we need codes that cover both software and hardware.

**Action Items:**
1. Register on **Startup India (DPIIT)** portal with NIC code **62011** as primary
2. Use **26309** as secondary code for IoT hardware
3. Get GST registration with SAC 998314 (software) + HSN 8543 (hardware)
4. Apply for **MSME/Udyam registration** — gives us tax benefits and easier bank loans

## Registration Roadmap

### Step 1: DPIIT Startup Recognition
- **Portal:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in
- **NIC Code:** 62011 (primary) + 26309 (secondary)
- **Benefits:** Tax exemption (Section 80-IAC), self-certification for labor/environment laws, fast-track patent filing, access to Fund of Funds

### Step 2: MSME/Udyam Registration
- **Portal:** https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- **Category:** Micro Enterprise (investment < ₹1 Cr, turnover < ₹5 Cr)
- **Benefits:** Priority sector lending, lower interest rates, government tender preference, subsidies

### Step 3: GST Registration
- **Required when:** Annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special states)
- **Register early** if selling IoT hardware (interstate supply)

### Step 4: Incorporation
- **Recommended:** Private Limited Company (for VC funding)
- **Alternative:** LLP (lower compliance, but harder to raise equity)
- **Portal:** https://www.mca.gov.in

## Important Notes

- **Primary NIC 62011** is the safest choice — most agri-tech and SaaS startups in India use this
- You can always add secondary codes later
- DPIIT recognition is **free** and takes 2-3 working days
- Keep your Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and company address proof ready
- The entity must be < 10 years old and turnover < ₹100 Cr to qualify as "startup"
