## The Problem

India has 120M+ small & medium farmers. Most lack access to:
- **Crop health data** — they can't afford satellite/drone monitoring
- **Expert advice** — government extension officers cover 1 per 1,000+ farmers
- **Soil data** — no real-time info on moisture, nutrients, or irrigation timing
- **Technology** — existing agri-tech solutions are expensive, English-only, and B2B focused

**Result:** Crop losses, water waste, low yields, and farmer debt — contributing to India's agrarian crisis.

## Our Solution

**Fasal Seva** is a mobile app + IoT hardware platform that gives every farmer access to smart farming technology — **for free**.

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| 🛰️ **Satellite Monitoring** | 5 vegetation indices (NDVI, NDWI, NDRE, SAVI, EVI) — see crop health from space |
| 🤖 **Sevak AI** | Voice-based AI assistant that speaks Hindi — ask any farming question, anytime |
| 📡 **IoT Sensors** | Real-time soil moisture, temperature & nutrient data via affordable device (₹3,499) |
| 💧 **Smart Irrigation** | AI-powered scheduling — tells farmers exactly when and how much to water |
| 🌦️ **Weather Alerts** | Hyper-local forecasts and disaster warnings |
| 📊 **Farm Dashboard** | All data in one place — simple enough for a first-time smartphone user |

<div class="highlight">
<strong>Key Differentiator:</strong> No competitor combines Voice AI (Hindi) + Satellite + IoT at this price point. Existing players are either B2B, English-only, expensive, or missing IoT/Voice altogether.
</div>
