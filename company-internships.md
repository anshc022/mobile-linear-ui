## 💡 TIPS

1. **On-campus vs Off-campus:** Top companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman) primarily hire via on-campus. If your college isn't on their list, apply via career portals and referrals.
2. **Referrals matter:** LinkedIn connect with employees → ask politely for referral.
3. **Competitive programming:** Practice on Codeforces, LeetCode, CodeChef — most companies test DSA heavily.
4. **Projects & open source:** Strong GitHub profile helps, especially for startups.
5. **Code for Good (JPMC):** Free hackathon → direct intern offers. Apply when it opens.
6. **Samsung PRISM:** Open research program for students — apply directly.
7. **Google Summer of Code (GSoC):** Not company-specific but excellent for open-source experience.
