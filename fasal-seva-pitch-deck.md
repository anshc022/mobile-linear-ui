### Slide 3: THE PROBLEM
**Title:** "120 million farmers. Blind decisions. ₹1.5 lakh crore in losses."

- **120M+ small/marginal farmers** (86% of Indian farms) make irrigation, fertilizer, and harvest decisions by intuition
- Existing precision ag tools cost **₹25,000-50,000+** and speak English
- Government Soil Health Cards: tested once in 2 years, arrive months late, in English
- **70% of target farmers** are semi-literate — text-based apps fail them
- **Result:** 20-30% preventable yield loss, ₹1.5 lakh crore/year

**The gap:** No solution combines satellite intelligence + AI voice + irrigation science for small farmers at an accessible price.

### Slide 5: HOW IT WORKS
**Title:** "Voice → Satellite → Science → Action"

1. **Farmer opens app** → WhatsApp OTP login (10 seconds)
2. **Maps their field** → tap-to-draw or walk the boundary (2 minutes)
3. **Asks Sevak Live** (in Hindi): *"Meri fasal ka kya haal hai?"*
4. **AI responds** with farm-specific advice, referencing:
   - Satellite imagery (5 indices, updated every 3-5 days)
   - FAO-56 irrigation calculations for their specific crop
   - 14-day weather forecast for their GPS location
   - IoT sensor data (if Fasal Seva Mini is installed)
5. **Farmer gets specific action:** "Irrigate Zone B tomorrow morning. Nitrogen levels in Zone A are low — apply 20kg urea."
6. **AR Zone Explorer** lets them see satellite overlay on their actual field through phone camera

## ❓ Anticipated Investor Questions

### Q: "You have 64 users. How do you know this isn't just early adopter noise?"
**A:** Fair question. 64 is early. But these are real farmers who found us organically on Play Store — no marketing, no incentives. Our 90-day plan is specifically designed to answer this: get to 500+ users, measure retention, and test willingness to pay. That's what the pre-seed is for.

### Q: "The app is free. How do you make money?"
**A:** We're testing three models: freemium subscription (₹49-99/month for premium features), IoT hardware sales (when manufacturing scales), and B2B data licensing. We intentionally kept it free to maximize initial adoption. Monetization testing starts in 30-60 days. We're transparent that this is unproven.

### Q: "Why should I invest at pre-revenue?"
**A:** Because the product risk is eliminated. We've built something that competitors with $20M+ couldn't. What remains is commercial risk — can we grow users and monetize? That's a much more solvable problem, and it's exactly what this round funds.

### Q: "What about the IoT sensor? Is that your real product?"
**A:** The sensor (Fasal Seva Mini) is a powerful add-on, but the app is the core product. 62 of our 64 users don't have sensors — they're using satellite monitoring, voice AI, and field mapping. Hardware is the premium layer for deeper precision. We'll scale it once manufacturing and unit economics are proven.

### Q: "Gemini 3.0 API costs — can you afford voice AI at scale?"
**A:** We need to measure this precisely (it's a week-1 priority). Our plan: free tier gets 5 voice calls/day, premium gets unlimited. For common queries ("should I irrigate?"), we can use cheaper models or rule-based logic. Gemini is reserved for complex, contextual conversations.

### Q: "What's your moat? Can a funded competitor copy this in 6 months?"
**A:** Our moat has four layers: (1) Feature depth — 9 modules already live, each deeply built; (2) Voice AI quality — Sevak Live's full-duplex conversations require deep integration with farm data, not just an API call; (3) Free pricing — competitors have business models that require charging, we can stay free longer; (4) Farmer data network effects — every field mapped, every satellite analysis, every voice conversation makes the system smarter.

### Q: "Why haven't you raised before now?"
**A:** We wanted to prove we could build first. Too many agritech startups raise on slides and struggle to ship. We shipped on zero budget. Now we need capital for things you can't bootstrap: hardware manufacturing, on-ground distribution, and sustained growth.

### Q: "What's your background? Do you understand farming?"
**A:** [Founder to personalize]. We've built deep agricultural domain knowledge into the product — FAO-56 irrigation science, 20+ crop profiles, 7 soil types. We're actively looking for an agri-domain co-founder or advisor to strengthen this.

## 🔥 Presentation Tips

1. **Lead with traction** — "64 users, zero funding" is your strongest opening. Don't bury it.
2. **Show the app** — Pull it up on Play Store live. Let investors see it's real.
3. **Be honest about what's not working** — "We don't have a revenue model yet" is strength, not weakness, when paired with "but we have a plan to test one."
4. **The feature comparison slide is your killer** — Show the table of what you have vs. what $20M-funded competitors have. Let the gap speak.
5. **Don't oversell hardware** — 2 sensor pilots is honest. Say it. "We're testing, not scaling."
6. **Know your numbers** — 64 users, 5 indices, FAO-56, 2 sensor pilots, 3 languages, ₹0 raised. Every number should be instant.
7. **End with the farmer** — "We didn't start with funding. We started with farmers."
