Smart Farming for Everyone

The Problem: Agriculture is Broken for the Small Farmer

Indian farmers are gambling with nature every season. While corporate farms use million-dollar tech, 86% of Indian farmers own less than 2 hectares and rely on guesswork, outdated almanacs, or expensive consultants they can't afford. They don't need another complex dashboard; they need an intelligent partner that speaks their language, works on their existing phones, and doesn't eat into their meager profits.

The Solution: Fasal Seva – The Voice-First "Super App"

We built Fasal Seva to democratize high-end agritech. It combines Real-Time Satellite Imagery (NDVI/health maps), IoT Soil Sensors, and a Voice-Native AI Assistant ("Sevak") into a single, free platform. A farmer simply opens the app and talks to it in Hindi or Tamil to diagnose pests, check soil moisture, or get weather alerts. By processing complex satellite data into simple, actionable voice advice, we bridge the digital divide instantly.

The Vision: Smart Kheti for Every Khet

Unlike competitors who charge subscription fees, Fasal Seva is 100% Free for Farmers, disrupting the traditional SaaS model. We leverage a unique hardware-software ecosystem where affordable IoT nodes calibrate our satellite models, creating a hyper-local data network. We aren't just building an app; we are building the operating system for the next Green Revolution, ensuring that data-driven farming is a right, not a luxury. "Ab Kheti Hogi Smart."
