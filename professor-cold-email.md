## 📝 Tips for Cold Emailing Professors

1. **Be specific:** Reference a particular paper or project. Generic emails get ignored.
2. **Keep it concise:** Professors are busy. 200-300 words max.
3. **Show competence:** Mention concrete projects, not just coursework names.
4. **Attach CV as PDF:** Name it `YourName_CV.pdf`.
5. **Follow up once:** If no reply in 10-14 days, send one polite follow-up.
6. **Apply broadly:** Email 15-25 professors across different IITs.
7. **Timing:** Send emails in Feb-March for summer positions.
8. **Subject line matters:** Include "Research Internship" + your year + area of interest.
9. **Find professors:** Check IIT department pages, Google Scholar, DBLP for active researchers.
10. **Don't mass-blast:** Personalize each email. Professors can tell when you haven't read their work.
