## 6. POSTING STRATEGY

### Best Time to Post
- **Morning 6-8 AM IST** — Farmers check phones before going to fields
- **Evening 7-9 PM IST** — After returning from fields, relaxing time
- **Sunday mornings** — Highest engagement (weekly market day in many areas)
- **Avoid:** 10 AM - 5 PM (farmers are in fields, low phone usage)

### What NOT to Do (Avoid Getting Banned)

1. **❌ Don't spam** — Never post the same message in multiple groups back-to-back. Admins talk to each other.
2. **❌ Don't post immediately after joining** — Lurk for 2-3 days, engage with existing posts first.
3. **❌ Don't use hard-sell language** — "BUY NOW", "BEST APP EVER" = instant ban
4. **❌ Don't post just links** — Always provide value alongside the link
5. **❌ Don't ignore group rules** — Read pinned messages, follow admin instructions
6. **❌ Don't post during inactive hours** — Wasted reach
7. **❌ Don't argue with critics** — If someone questions the app, respond politely with facts

### What TO Do (Growth Strategy)

1. **✅ Be a helpful member first** — Answer farming questions, share weather updates, THEN occasionally mention the app
2. **✅ Use voice messages** — Many farmers prefer audio over text (literacy varies)
3. **✅ Share screenshots** — Show the app interface, mandi prices, weather info
4. **✅ Get testimonials** — Ask early users to share their experience in groups
5. **✅ Partner with admins** — DM group admins, offer them something (early access, mention in app)
6. **✅ Create your own groups** — "Fasal Seva किसान परिवार" — build your community
7. **✅ Regional language content** — Hindi is primary but also try Marathi, Punjabi, Telugu, Tamil versions
8. **✅ ShareChat is underrated** — Most agri-tech startups ignore it, but rural India lives there
9. **✅ YouTube Shorts in Hindi** — Quick farming tips with Fasal Seva branding → massive reach
10. **✅ Refer-a-farmer program** — Word of mouth is king in villages

### Prioritized Platform Strategy
1. **WhatsApp Groups** — Direct, personal, high trust (START HERE)
2. **ShareChat** — Highest rural penetration, underused by competitors
3. **YouTube Shorts** — Viral potential, Hindi farming content
4. **Facebook Groups** — Large communities, good for detailed posts
5. **Telegram** — Growing, good for broadcast updates
6. **Koo App** — Hindi-first, farmer-friendly
